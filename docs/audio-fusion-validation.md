# Audio source policy and validation limits

The installed source policy is the previously frozen OpenMIC score head identified by `src/audio/qualifiedFusionRelease.ts` and the hash-checked files in `public/fusion-model/`. Its accepted input tier is a complete ten-second Ogg window with the qualified isolated WASM runtime (AST/CLAP four threads, Jamendo one). Unsupported inputs, devices and failures retain native estimates. Imported analysis cannot acquire a local qualification receipt.

The historical 256-recording diagnostic cohort had precision 72.86%, recall 89.91% and F1 80.49% on sparsely observed annotations (10.35% label coverage). That cohort has been consumed and informed subsequent development. Its acceptance receipt does **not** establish 90% precision and 90% recall per category on representative unseen audio. No category is certified to that requirement, which also requires both lower 95% confidence bounds to reach 90% under independent recording-family testing. Unannotated classes remain unknown rather than negative; effects, sound character, BPM and key have no accuracy claim from this source dataset.

Later fusion/temporal/PaSST comparisons remain research. The PaSST adapter is explicitly default-off (`VITE_EXPERIMENTAL_PASST=1` is needed for a local research build), has no registered deployment validation receipt, bypasses persistent analysis caches, and restores the installed detector on failure. Its checkpoint and ONNX export are not included in this repository release. PaSST checkpoint redistribution rights and foundation-training overlap remain unresolved, so its calibration scores are not independent test results. No blind reserve was used in these comparisons.
