Generated by `scripts/tag-decision-map.ts` (main at `f271b7a`) from `src/audio/djTags.ts`, `src/audio/taggerPolicy.json`, `public/sound-model/learned.json`, `public/sound-model/short-clip.json`, `public/sound-model/prompts.json` and the display policy in `src/audio/confidentSoundSummary.ts`. Rerun it when any of those files change (`npx vite-node scripts/tag-decision-map.ts`; `--check` only verifies): the table is read straight from them, nothing in it is typed by hand.

## How a tag gets onto the screen (plain version)

The app has three detectors plus one fallback, and a display rule that picks between them:

1. **Trained tagger** (`src/audio/tagger.ts`, ONNX model `cmjatom/dge-all-tags-tagger-nc`). It listens to whole 10 s windows at 32 kHz (one padded window for a short clip, the middle 30 s of a long song). A tag in `taggerPolicy.json` shows when the best window's score passes that tag's cutoff. Cutoffs are applied when the track is shown, so changing one needs no re-analysis. On recordings longer than 30 s most entries hand the tag back to the other detectors (`long: detectors`). Inside the windows it heard, the tagger **replaces** every other detector for the labels in its `decides` list (the display drops their scores there). The model also scores 166 catalog labels that are not in the policy (kick, clap, hi-hat, snare, ...); the app never reads them: `TaggerEvidence` keeps policy outputs only and `taggerDecisions` walks the policy, so an unlisted output such as `cat:hi-hat` cannot show a tag until a policy entry names it.
2. **CLAP trained heads** (`learned.json`, read by `learnedDjScores`). Every 10 s CLAP window (hop 5 s, repeat-padded when shorter) gets a 512-number fingerprint; each head is a logistic regression on that fingerprint with its own cutoff. A head that fires in any window adds its tag; "maybe" heads show faded. The reviewed-example path in the same file holds 0 examples today.
3. **One-shot heads** (`short-clip.json`). Only for whole clips of at most 2.25 s. On those clips the regular CLAP heads are switched off unless marked `oneShot` (vinyl scratch, impact, laser), and these 20 heads decide instead.
4. **Raw CLAP prompts** (`prompts.json`, picked by `selectDjTags`): cosine similarity to text prompts, compared within an axis (top prompt must beat its cutoff and lead the runner-up by a margin). The display shows such a tag only as a faded "possible" on clips longer than 2.25 s when the similarity is at least 0.5, and never for a label that already has a tested detector (`calibratedLabels.json`) or that was measured as almost always wrong (`unverifiedBlocked.json`).

Merged names (`MERGED_DJ_LABELS`) are folded at display time: a detector for `closed hi-hat` shows as **hi-hat**, `bass hit` / `bass pluck` / `rubbery bass` show as **synth bass**, `turntable` shows as **vinyl scratch**, and so on. The table lists those under the surviving name. Picker-hidden tags (`DJ_HIDDEN_LABELS`) still run and show on clips; they are just not listed until searched for. 2 labels (`distorted`, `reverse effect`) are hidden from every model until a detector passes real clips. Nine "feel" words (warm, airy, metallic, ...) come from DSP rules, never from a model.

Display floors: a tested score shows at 0.4 or more on recordings longer than 2.25 s and 0.5 or more on one-shots; every head cutoff is at least 0.5, so in practice "head fired" means "tag shown". Tagger scores are rescaled so the cutoff reads as 0.5.

## Known precedence and traps

- **Tagger beats CLAP inside its windows.** For a tag with both (drums, synthesizer, foley, bell, percussion, ...), the CLAP head only matters on audio the tagger did not score: recordings longer than 30 s (or 10 s for `max`-rule tags without a long rule) when the entry says `long: detectors`, and recordings shorter than `minSeconds`.
- **One-shots are a separate world.** On a clip of 2.25 s or less (most drum hits), kick / clap / hi-hat / snare / cymbal are decided by `short-clip.json`, not by `learned.json`. The learned kick head never runs on a one-shot kick sample; the one-shot kick head does. "synth bass" on a one-shot comes only from the `bass hit` one-shot head through the merge.
- **hi-hat** is decided by the tagger on recordings up to 30 s (cutoff 0.3663, PR #234); on longer recordings only the merged `closed hi-hat` maybe head remains, so a long hi-hat loop still has essentially no detector.
- **synth bass** on clips longer than 2.25 s: no head, prompt blocked. It cannot show today except through a listener's own confirmation.
- **downlifter** on screen is the tagger's `cat:falling` output (merged from `production:downlifter`); the CLAP `falling` head only applies outside the tagger's windows.
- **Clip length changes the brain**: a 2 s kick is decided by the one-shot head, a 4 s kick by the learned head, a 40 s drum loop also by the learned head (no tagger entry). Any measurement must therefore use clips of the lengths users upload.

## How to measure a tag the way the app decides it

`scripts/eval-tag.ts` runs this exact chain (CLAP windows, heads, one-shot heads, prompt selection, tagger windows, then `confidentSoundSummary`) on labelled clips and reports precision, recall and false-alarm rate at the current cutoffs plus a cutoff sweep for each candidate brain. See the header of that script for usage.
