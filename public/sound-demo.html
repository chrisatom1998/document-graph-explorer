        How does it decide? (plain English)

        It measures a handful of things about a clip, then checks how well they match a short description of each sound type.

        • How fast it starts. A drum hit reaches full volume almost instantly. A pad fades in slowly.
        • How long it lasts. Hats and kicks are over in a blink. Pads and bass notes hang around.
        • Deep or bright. How much of the sound is deep bass (below 150 Hz) versus airy treble (above 6,000 Hz).
        • Musical or noisy. A note concentrates its energy at a few pitches. Noise spreads it everywhere.
        • Building up. Risers get louder and brighter toward the end.

        Seven sound types are supported here: kick, bass, snare/clap, hi-hat, pluck/lead, pad and riser. Anything else, like vocals, full songs or exotic instruments, may come back as "unclear". That is on purpose: saying "I can't tell" beats a confident wrong answer.
